FAQ
Common questions
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 2–4 working days across India. Express next-day delivery is available in Mumbai and Delhi.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 15 days of delivery. Items must be unworn with original tags. Refunds are processed in 3–5 working days.
Do you have a size guide?
Yes! Each product page has a detailed size guide. For kids, we size by age and height. For men, we follow standard Indian sizing (S to 3XL).
Can I track my order?
Yes — you'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also track via the link in your account.
